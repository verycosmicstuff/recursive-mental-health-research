🚀 Mission & Overview

This project is an autonomous recursive self-improvement loop designed to discover the most clinically effective conversational strategies for text-based mental health support.

By deploying local LLMs inside a structured state machine, the system acts as an evolutionary laboratory. It tests variations of therapist system prompts against simulated patients, evaluates the results, and propagates the successful techniques to subsequent generations.

🔄 The Research Loop

1. Generate Create synthetic patient archetype profile
2. Simulate Conduct 5-15 turn therapy session
3. Evaluate Score clinically & somatic shift
4. Evolve Agent rewrites therapist prompt

🎯 Project Goals

  • ✓ Clinical Optimization: Maximize patient transition to safe emotional baseline (Ventral Vagal shift).
  • ✓ Brevity & Safety: Build prompts that maintain clear clinical boundaries and keep therapist responses concise without losing empathy.
  • ✓ Automatic Restructuring: Allow LLMs to self-correct clinical weaknesses (like premature problem-solving or pseudo-insight).

📚 Clinical Frameworks & Glossary

PCT (Person-Centered Therapy)

Originating from Carl Rogers, PCT emphasizes unconditional positive regard, congruence, and active validation. The therapist mirrors the patient's feelings and experiences verbatim or paraphrased to build a deep therapeutic alliance, avoiding any evaluation or advice.

ACT (Acceptance & Commitment)

ACT focuses on psychological flexibility. Rather than trying to change or fight negative thoughts, patients practice cognitive defusion (observing thoughts as just words) and clarify their core values to take committed, values-aligned micro-actions.

CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)

CBT works on identifying and restructuring cognitive distortions (Automatic Negative Thoughts or ANTs). The therapist guides the patient to evaluate evidence for and against a belief, creating a more balanced, alternative thought.

Socratic Questioning

A form of guided discovery where the therapist asks structured, open-ended questions instead of offering direct solutions. This forces the patient to self-reflect and draw their own healthy conclusions, building long-term agency.

Somatic State Mapping

Grounded in Polyvagal theory, patient responses are mapped into three autonomic states:
🔴 Sympathetic: Fight or flight (anxiety, frustration).
🔵 Dorsal Vagal: Freeze or shutdown (numbness, isolation).
🟢 Ventral Vagal: Grounded safety (calm, social connection).

Adversarial Auditing

A secondary scorer that monitors the session. If the therapist uses forbidden clinical shortcuts (such as premature advice, claiming to be human, or violating boundaries), it triggers a penalty multiplier to reduce the final experiment score.
